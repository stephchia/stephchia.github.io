I contributed to analyses of citizen science occurrence data and GPS-tracked movement data to investigate animal movement behaviors.

- **Altitudinal migration of birds**: Using eBird data to characterize seasonal elevational movement, we found that the majority of resident birds in Taiwan shift elevation across seasons, likely driven by thermal tolerance and food availability ([Tsai et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1111/ecog.05196)).
- **Route navigation of carnivores**: Analyzing GPS data from over 1,000 individuals across 34 species, we found distinct differences between wild cats and dogs in how they navigate their home ranges ([Fagan et al. 2025](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2401042122)).
