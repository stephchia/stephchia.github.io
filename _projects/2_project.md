Traits shape how organisms interact with their environments and each other. I study how trait variations across space are shaped by biotic and abiotic pressures.

- **Bird nest architectures**: By integrating nest traits and environmental variables across global birds, we tested whether dome and cavity nests serve to reduce predation risk or regulate nest microclimate ([Chia et al. in press]()).
- **Moth body size across elevations**: Using changes in moth body size composition along an elevational gradient, we investigated how climate influences the relative importance of environmental filtering and niche partitioning in shaping insect assemblages ([Chia et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14467)).
